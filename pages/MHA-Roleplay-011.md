The softball sails across the dirt in a clean, textbook arc, hits the ground with a dull thump, and rolls to a stop.

Beep.

Aizawa turns the digital screen around to face the class. In crisp black numerals, it reads: 76.5m.

For two full seconds, nobody makes a sound.

"Huh? Seventy-six point five?" a blond student with a black lightning streak in his hair blinks, leaning out from the line. "Didn't Mr. Aizawa just say he got first place in the entrance exam?"

"Yeah, that's just a normal good throw!" a spiky redhead beside him adds in confusion, while Midoriya hurriedly whispers to Uraraka that Khadar's Quirk must not be a physical strength enhancer given how he fought with scavenged robot cables during the exam.

Three paces away, Bakugo lets out a harsh, barking laugh that sounds half-way to a snarl, small pops of orange sparks cracking across his palms. "HAH?! Seventy-six meters?! You've gotta be kidding me—a weak-ass normal throw from the bastard who stole first place with charity points?! Get out of the circle, extra!"

Unbothered, Aizawa pockets the monitor and pulls a second softball from his jumpsuit. His tired eyes linger on Khadar for a second as Khadar rubs the back of his neck.

"A purely reactive or cognitive Quirk won't boost your baseline physical output in a vacuum," Aizawa remarks flatly, making a quick mental note before tossing the second ball toward Bakugo. "Bakugo. You had the top villain score at seventy-seven. Show them what happens when an emitter Quirk is applied."

Bakugo snatches the ball out of the air, stalks past Khadar into the chalk circle, and winds his arm back low.

"DIE!!"

With a deafening BOOM of nitroglycerin-like blast wave, the softball rockets into the blue sky like a surface-to-air missile, leaving a trail of black smoke across the pitch.

Beep. Aizawa holds up the second reading: 705.2m.

"Know your own maximum first," Aizawa says as the class erupts into amazed gasps. "That is the most rational way to form the foundation of a hero."

"705 meters? Seriously?!"

"What's this? It looks fun!" Mina Ashido cheers, pumping her fists.

"We can use our Quirks as much as we want! As expected from the hero course!" Hanta Sero grins.

Aizawa's head tilts down, his messy black hair falling across his brow.

"'It looks fun,' huh?" His voice drops half an octave. "You have three years to become a hero. Will you have an attitude like that the whole time? All right. Whoever comes in last place in all eight tests will be judged to have no potential, and will be punished with expulsion."

"HUH?!"

A collective jolt runs through the group. Several students immediately glance around the crowd and count heads—realizing with a sudden chill that there are twenty-one of them standing here for a twenty-seat homeroom.

"Last place will be expelled?! Walked in on the first day?!" Uraraka protests, stepping forward with her hands balled into fists. "Even if it wasn't the first day—that's too unfair!"

Aizawa sweeps his dark hair back from his face, revealing a wide, unsettling grin beneath his bloodshot eyes.

"Natural disasters, big accidents, and selfish villains. Calamities whose time or place can't be predicted. Japan is covered with unfairness. Heroes are the ones who reverse those situations. If you wanted to go talk with your friends at Mickey D's after school, too bad. For the next three years, U.A. will do all it can to give you one hardship after another. Go beyond—Plus Ultra. Overcome it with all you've got."

Walking over to a fifty-meter track lined out on the dirt with a camera-equipped robotic timer stationed at the finish line, Aizawa gestures the class forward.

First up, Iida blasts across the fifty-meter line in 3.04 seconds with his calf engines, while Uraraka Lightens her clothes and shoes for 7.15 seconds, and Aoyama propels himself backward with his Navel Laser for 5.51 seconds.

Finally, Aizawa glances down at his clipboard for the final three-lane heat of Test 1: The 50-Meter Dash.

"Last group—Bakugo, Midoriya, Khadar. Step up to the starting line."

On the left lane, Bakugo drops into a predatory crouch with his palms angled back like twin thrusters; in the middle lane, Midoriya swallows hard and lowers himself into a tense runner's stance, sweating bullets now that he knows baseline physical scores could mean getting cut as the twenty-first student; leaving the right-hand lane open for Khadar as the robotic starter raises its flag: "On your marks... Get set..."
