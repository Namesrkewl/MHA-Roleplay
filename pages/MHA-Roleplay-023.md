### Model

Yaoyorozu nods, flips the siren switch for a sharp, two-tone electronic chirp that slices cleanly over the building klaxon, and raises the bullhorn to her lips.

"EVERYONE, STOP PUSHING AND LISTEN! EVERYTHING IS FINE!!"

Her amplified voice thunders down the length of the packed hallway. Instantly, five hundred panicked students freeze in their tracks, heads snapping toward the window where Yaoyorozu stands flanked by Khadar and Iida.

"LOOK OUT THE COURTYARD WINDOWS! IT IS ONLY THE PRESS CORPS OUTSIDE THE FRONT GATE! THERE IS NO NEED TO PANIC! WE ARE AT U.A.—LET US CONDUCT OURSELVES IN A MANNER BEFITTING THE BEST!"

Right beside her, Iida raises a rigid arm toward the tall panes of glass. "Look for yourselves—the faculty and police are already escorting the reporters out! Please return to the cafeteria in an orderly fashion!"

Within seconds, a wave of collective relief washes through the corridor as upperclassmen and first-years alike peer out the glass, sheepishly apologizing to the people they were just shoving as the crush disperses.

"Whoa, pulling a megaphone out of thin air stopped the whole stampede in five seconds flat!" Uraraka breathes as she and Midoriya make their way over through the thinning crowd. "You two really are the ultimate Class Rep and Deputy Rep team!"

"Indeed!" Iida adjusts his glasses with earnest respect. "Quick thinking from Khadar to identify the simplest solution, and immediate execution from Yaoyorozu! Our class is in thoroughly capable hands!"

At 12:50 p.m., back in Classroom 1-A after the police finish clearing the pulverized remains of the front gate, Aizawa steps up to the homeroom lectern.

"For today's Hero Basic Training, it's turned into a class with three instructors," Aizawa announces. "All Might and me, and one more person."

"Excuse me! What'll we be doing?" Sero raises a hand.

Aizawa holds up a printed card reading RESCUE. "Disasters, shipwrecks, and everything in between. It's rescue training. It's up to each of you whether or not you wear your costume this time, since some gear limits movement. The training will take place off-campus, so we're taking a bus. That is all. Get ready."

Twenty minutes later, suited up in their Hero Costumes—with Midoriya wearing his P.E. top alongside his support gear since his green jumpsuit was torn yesterday—Class 1-A boards the wide, open-layout bus rolling through the hills toward the off-campus facility.

With Yaoyorozu and Khadar handling the headcount as Class Rep and Deputy Rep—much to the disappointment of Iida, who had brought a referee whistle just in case—the students settle into the facing bench rows along the walls of the bus.

Across the aisle from Khadar and Yaoyorozu, Tsu sits next to Midoriya, Kirishima, and Kaminari.

"Midoriya, I always say whatever's on my mind," Tsu says bluntly, turning her large eyes on him. "Your Quirk is like All Might's, isn't it?"

"H-huh?! Y-you think so?! Wait—I'm—!" Midoriya panics, waving his hands frantically before Kirishima leans forward and saves him without realizing it.

"Wait a sec, Tsu, All Might doesn't get hurt when he uses his! They just look the same," Kirishima says, before sighing and hardening his left arm into jagged rock. "Still, it's nice to have a simple augmenting-type Quirk that's flashy! Think about it—if you're going to be a pro, you want something flashy! My Hardening's strong against others in a fight, but unfortunately, it doesn't look Like much."

"I think it's really cool, though!" Midoriya insists. "And if we're talking about people who don't need flashy emitter Quirks to dominate, look at Khadar—his Quirk is just slow-motion perception, and with his Switch-Links yesterday he didn't even let you and Sero throw a punch!"

"Don't remind me, bro, I'm still recovering from that!" Kirishima laughs, before gesturing down the bus row. "Though if we're talking about flashy and strong for a pro, it's definitely Todoroki and Bakugo, right?"

Tsu glances toward the back where Bakugo sits scowling out the window. "Bakugo's always mad, so he doesn't seem like he'll be very popular, ribbit."

"WHAT DID YOU SAY, FROG-FACE?! I'LL KICK YOUR ASS!!" Bakugo vaults halfway over the handrail, exploding at the palm.

"See?" Tsu points a calm finger at him.

"We haven't even known each other a week, and everyone already knows your personality is flaming garbage steeped in sewage," Kaminari chuckles, leaning back in his seat as Bakugo threatens to murder him next, while beside Khadar, Yaoyorozu presses two fingers to her temple and glances at her Deputy Rep with a quiet sigh at the noise level.
